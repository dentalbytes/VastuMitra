Vastu for Offices

The same underlying directional principles apply to commercial spaces, adapted for how offices actually function.

👔 CEO / Owner's Cabin

Best in the South-West, seated facing East or North with a solid wall behind — never a window or door.

Avoid: sitting directly under a beam, or facing a bathroom/pantry wall.

💰 Cash Counter / Accounts

Best in the North (Kubera's direction), with the cash box or safe opening toward the North.

Avoid: a cash counter directly facing the main entrance.

🛎️ Reception

Best near the North or East entrance, with the receptionist facing East or North.

Avoid: a cluttered, dark, or poorly lit reception area.

🗣️ Conference Room

Best in the West or North-West, with the most senior person facing East or North during meetings.

Avoid: a conference table positioned directly under a beam.

☕ Pantry

Best in the South-East (the fire corner), same logic as a home kitchen.

Avoid: placing it in the North-East.

💻 Workstations

Employees ideally face East or North while working; management/senior staff toward the South or West of the floor.

Avoid: desks facing a wall with no space ahead, or seated with their back to the main door.

A note on shared/leased offices

Most offices lease a fixed floor plan, so — like the Rent House Checker — the practical lever is usually desk and furniture orientation rather than structural change. The Remedies Library has non-structural suggestions that apply just as well to a commercial space.
