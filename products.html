Product Guide

What these common remedy objects are traditionally used for, and when they're actually relevant — this is an educational guide, not a shop, and we're not recommending any specific brand, seller, or price.

Only reach for one of these when it addresses a specific placement issue you've identified (via the Room Checker or Remedies Library) — more objects doesn't mean more "Vastu compliant." A cluttered home works against most Vastu principles regardless of what's in it.

🔺 Copper Pyramids

When it's suggested

To correct a room or corner that's in a traditionally weaker direction (e.g. the South-East of a bedroom), typically placed in that corner.

🪴 Plants

When it's suggested

General wellbeing and air quality, or to "soften" a difficult corner. See the full Plants & Trees guide for which species and where.

🎐 Wind Chimes

When it's suggested

Near entrances or in the North-West, traditionally believed to keep energy moving. Metal chimes are most commonly used.

🧂 Himalayan Salt Lamps

When it's suggested

For rooms in traditionally "heavier" placements (like a North-East bathroom or kitchen), typically kept lit for a few hours a day.

🐢 Brass Tortoise

When it's suggested

Traditionally placed facing North in the living room or entrance, associated with stability and slow, steady progress.

💎 Crystals

When it's suggested

Clear quartz near a work desk, or citrine near a cash counter, are common traditional choices — more a matter of personal/family custom than a fixed rule.
