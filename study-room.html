Study Room

For studying and mental focus, Vastu draws on the same logic used for the pooja room — the North-East is considered good for calm, clear thinking, while facing East or North while working is thought to support concentration.

Ideal Direction

The North, East, or North-East part of the house is preferred for a study room, and the study desk is traditionally positioned so the person sits facing East (for knowledge and focus) or North (for career and opportunity).

Traditionally Recommended

  • Face East or North while studying or working at a desk.
  • Place bookshelves along the South or West walls.
  • Keep the desk clear of clutter, with good natural light from the East if possible.
  • Sit with solid wall support behind you, rather than a window or open door.
  • Use light, calming wall colors like white, light green, or light blue.

Traditionally Avoided

  • Studying while facing South.
  • Sitting directly under an exposed ceiling beam.
  • A study desk facing directly into a wall with no space ahead.
  • A cluttered or dark study space with poor ventilation.
  • Placing the study area in the South-West, traditionally reserved for rest rather than mental activity.

North Indian vs. South Indian view

The study room isn't always treated as a distinct category in older Vastu texts, since traditional homes in both regions historically combined study/work with the pooja room or a general living space. Modern interpretations — common to both North and South Indian practitioners today — largely extend the same North-East and East-facing logic used for prayer and mental clarity. There isn't a strong, well-documented regional split specific to this room the way there is for entrances or staircases.
